Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

Overview

PingPay ("we," "us," or "our") provides a billing management and SMS notification service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information about our customers and their clients in connection with our services.

By using PingPay, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our billing and notification services:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, and email address of service clients
  • Billing information — invoice amounts, due dates, payment status, and payment method (Zelle, Apple Pay, Cash, or Card)
  • Service plan details — plan name, pricing, and billing cycle
  • SMS communications — records of inbound and outbound text messages sent through our platform
  • Payment records — dates, amounts, and methods of payments received

We do not collect Social Security numbers, full credit card numbers, or bank account numbers.

SMS Messaging

📱 Message & Data Rates May Apply. By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS messages from PingPay related to your account, billing reminders, payment confirmations, and account notifications.

We use SMS messaging for the following purposes:

  • Payment due date reminders
  • Overdue payment notices
  • Payment confirmation receipts
  • Account status notifications
  • Responses to account inquiries sent via SMS

Opt-Out: You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To opt back in, reply START.

Help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance or contact us at the information below.

Message frequency varies based on your billing cycle and account activity. We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send billing reminders and payment notifications via SMS
  • Process and record payments
  • Maintain accurate billing records
  • Respond to account inquiries
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored securely on self-hosted infrastructure with restricted access. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

SMS messages are sent via Twilio, a trusted communications platform. Twilio's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy available at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

Card payments are processed via Stripe. Stripe's privacy practices are governed by their Privacy Policy at stripe.com/privacy. We do not store full card numbers.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Billing records are retained for a minimum of 3 years for accounting purposes. SMS logs are retained for up to 1 year.

You may request deletion of your personal information by contacting us directly. Note that we may be required to retain certain records for legal or accounting purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP
  • Contact us with questions or concerns about your data

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate PingPay:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery and phone number services
  • Stripe — Card payment processing (optional)
  • Cloudflare — DNS, security, and content delivery

Each of these providers has their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify affected clients of significant changes. The date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last updated.

Contact Us
